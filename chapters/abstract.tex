		\noindent Inertial sensors are commonly used in navigation and mobile robots. These sensors are highly robust and can function effectively in nearly all conditions. However, their lack of precision significantly limits their application as measurement systems in stationary mechanisms. Despite this limitation, a promising niche for inertial-based position and orientation estimation exists in constrained multi-body systems. An approach that integrates an Inertial Measurement Unit (IMU) with mechanism-structure awareness to enhance estimation accuracy were presented. The proposed method leverages an Extended Kalman Filter (EKF) with an improved constraint correction technique. By incorporating structural constraints directly into the estimation process, the method effectively reduces errors associated with conventional inertial navigation systems. Proposed algorithm were implemented and validated. The study highlights the potential of structure-aware sensor fusion techniques in improving inertial navigation in constrained environments.
		
		
	\end{abstract}
